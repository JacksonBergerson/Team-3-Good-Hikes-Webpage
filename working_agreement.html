Team Working Agreement

Values, Communication Standards & Team Preferences

Team Values & Ways of Working

  1. Meeting Formats & Schedule Outside of Class: We will hold our regular team meetings outside of class via Zoom on Tuesday and Thursday. If an agenda requires working around different schedules, calls will be scheduled in advance with a clear agenda, and notes or recordings will be posted for anyone unable to attend synchronously.
  2. Availability Times & Communication Norms: All team members agree to follow the 24-hour response rule across our communication platforms. We recognize that everyone has different prime working hours (such as afternoons or late evenings), but acknowledge messages promptly so tasks never get blocked.
  3. Transparent Communication: Update the team on Basecamp whenever you make changes or add new assets. Always leave descriptive comments on your work so everyone knows what was updated and why.
  4. Accountability & Ownership: Each member takes full ownership of their assigned tasks and deliverables. If someone encounters an unexpected delay or obstacle, they agree to notify the group early rather than waiting until the deadline.
  5. Punctuality: Be on time for all scheduled Zoom meetings and meet agreed-upon project milestones. Starting calls promptly respects everyone's busy schedules.
  6. Independence: Team members are empowered to manage their own time and work independently on their sections, trusting each other to complete their parts before group review.
  7. Quality & Functionality: We strive to produce work that is not only complete but also functional, thoroughly checked, and meeting all project requirements before submission.
  8. Constructive Feedback & Open Questions: Never hesitate to ask a question, and be willing to answer teammates' questions. All critiques should remain kind, constructive, and focused on improving the final project.

Our Communication Channels

  • Basecamp: Primary hub for project updates, task management, file sharing, and leaving comments on ongoing work.
  • Tuesday / Thursday Zoom Sessions: Face-to-face check-ins, sprint reviews, and synchronous problem solving.
  • Team Group Chat: Quick questions, meeting reminders, and urgent heads-up notices.
