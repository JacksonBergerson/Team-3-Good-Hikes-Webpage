Our Difficulty Rating System

Our difficulty system is based off of the Petzoldt Energy Mile theory which, in the simplest terms for our purposes, means that for each 500ft you ascend, you are exerting yourself the same amount as if you walked one mile on flat ground. Our system is on a 1-10 scale for easy comprehension. Under the hood is a points-based system, with a mile and 500ft of ascension each being worth 1 point, with different point thresholds for where the hikes land on the 1-10 scale. For example, a 5/10 difficulty hike requires 9 or 10 points, which could correlate to a hike like Skyline Loop, which has a length around 6 miles and an elevation gain of around 1,500ft. Our system also accounts for terrain and weather (IP) with their own respective point amounts.

Factors accounted for:

  • Altitude
  • Distance
  • Terrain
  • Weather
